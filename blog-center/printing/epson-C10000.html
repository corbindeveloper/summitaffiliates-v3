    Epson WorkForce Enterprise AM-C10000 - High Volume Color MFP Built for Modern Business

    What if the biggest drain on your office's productivity and energy budget wasn't your software, your internet connection, or even your team — but your printer?

    Most businesses never stop to question it. The printer hums along, the toner gets replaced, the electricity bill arrives, and nobody bats an eye. But underneath that workflow, there is a better way to print — one that Epson has been engineering for years and comes packed in its modern machines.

    This is known as PrecisionCore Heat-Free Technology, and it is the foundation of the brand-new Epson WorkForce Enterprise AM-C10000 Color Multifunction Printer. Once you understand how it works and what it means for your business, you will wonder how you ever settled for anything less.


    AM Series Overview Video - By Epson America


    What Is Epson PrecisionCore Heat-Free Technology — and Why Does It Matter?


    Traditional office printing relies on heat. A lot of it. The printing process requires a fuser unit to reach extreme temperatures to bond toner to paper, which means every print job demands a warm-up cycle, a surge of electricity, and a steady stream of heat pumping into your workspace.

    Epson's PrecisionCore Heat-Free technology throws that entire process out the window.

    Instead of using heat to drive the printing process, PrecisionCore uses precision-engineered piezoelectric elements to fire microscopic droplets of ink with extraordinary accuracy — no heat required, no fuser, no warm-up time. The result is a printing experience that is faster to start, cheaper to run, and more reliable over the long haul. Here is what that looks like in practice:

    • Zero warm-up time — the AM-C10000 is ready to print the instant you need it, with a first-page-out time of just 4.7 seconds
    • Up to 80% lower power consumption compared to color laser printers in the same class
    • Fewer consumable parts — with no fuser unit or drum to replace, service intervals are longer and intervention is dramatically reduced
    • Cooler, quieter operation — no heat means no fan surges, no warm exhaust air, and a noticeably calmer presence in your office environment
    • A Typical Energy Consumption (TEC) value of just 0.30 kWh — among the lowest in its class

    This is not a marginal improvement. It is a fundamental reimagining of how enterprise printing works, and the AM-C10000 is its most powerful expression yet.


    Built on That Foundation: 100 Pages Per Minute, Every Time


    PrecisionCore Heat-Free technology does not just make the AM-C10000 efficient — it makes it fast. Genuinely, impressively fast.

    The AM-C10000 delivers 100 pages per minute (ppm) in both full color and monochrome, and it maintains that speed for duplex (double-sided) printing as well. There is no trade-off between color and speed, no slowdown for two-sided jobs, and no waiting for the machine to find its rhythm. It simply prints, continuously, at 100 ppm from the moment the first page hits the tray.

    For high-volume office environments, print shops, and corporate reprographics departments, that kind of consistent throughput is transformational. Print queues clear faster, staff spend less time waiting, and the machine earns its place as a true productivity engine rather than a bottleneck.

    Professional Color Quality That Speaks for Itself


    Speed and efficiency are only part of the story. The AM-C10000 pairs its remarkable performance with output quality that is genuinely impressive for an office-class device.

    It uses Epson's DURABrite Pro pigment inks, which are engineered to produce rapid-drying, water-resistant prints with vivid color accuracy and crisp, sharp black text. Pigment ink sits on the surface of the paper rather than soaking in, which means documents resist smudging, stand up to highlighter pens, and maintain a professional appearance even after heavy handling.

    For businesses where brand color accuracy is critical — think letterheads, branded envelopes, or client-facing documents — the optional Epson Edge Print software takes things further, offering PANTONE color matching, ICC color profile control, and spot color support to ensure every job matches the exact specification.


    A Full Packaged Multifunction Powerhouse


    The AM-C10000 is far more than a printer. It is a full-featured document hub designed to handle every stage of the office document lifecycle:

    Print

    • 100 ppm color and monochrome output across A4, A3, and up to SRA3 media
    • Support for envelopes, tracing paper, and media weights from 52 to 350 g/m²
    • Optional Epson Edge Print for production-grade color management

    Copy & Scan

    • Copy speeds up to 100 cpm in color and monochrome
    • Duplex scan speeds up to 120 images per minute via the 150-sheet ADF
    • Scan-to-email, scan-to-FTP, scan-to-USB, and cloud-based scanning
    • Output in PDF, JPEG, TIFF, PNG, and more

    Finishing & Media Handling

    • Up to 5,350 sheets of total paper input across multiple trays
    • Optional automated stapling, hole punching, and saddle stitching
    • A3+ high-capacity tray for uninterrupted large-volume runs

    Connectivity & Workflow

    • Gigabit Ethernet, Wi-Fi (IEEE 802.11a/b/g/n), and Wi-Fi Direct
    • Apple AirPrint, Mopria Print Service, and Mopria Scan
    • Epson Solutions Suite plus Epson Open Platform for third-party integrations
    • Intuitive 10.1-inch color touchscreen for easy, smartphone-like operation

    Security

    • PIN code secure print release
    • LDAP Address Book, IP address filtering, and IPsec
    • IEEE 802.1X authentication and SSL/TLS encrypted communications

    Who Is the Epson AM-C10000 Built For?


    The AM-C10000 is the right fit for any organization that demands high performance, low running costs, and professional results. It is particularly well-suited for:

    • Corporate offices and in-house reprographics departments that need to produce large volumes of branded, presentation-quality documents without outsourcing
    • Print shops and copy centers requiring precise color accuracy and consistent output across long production runs
    • Managed Print Service (MPS) providers looking to offer clients a high-speed, low-maintenance, energy-efficient fleet solution
    • Organizations with active ESG commitments that need to reduce their energy footprint without compromising on productivity
